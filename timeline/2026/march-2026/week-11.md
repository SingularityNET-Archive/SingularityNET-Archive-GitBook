#### Narrative:
The meeting focused on clarifying how the Ambassador Program should approach project selection and proposal development for the upcoming Q2 budget, while also reflecting on the broader direction of the program. Participants noted that there had been ongoing uncertainty about who should be responsible for proposing projects. This was clarified during the discussion when Vanessa emphasized that there are no individual proposers; instead, the community collectively develops proposals. The goal is to collaboratively craft a single proposal that the group can move forward to the consent process with, rather than presenting multiple competing proposals and asking members to vote between them.

The group reflected on the fact that project ideas had been requested for over eight weeks, yet there was still difficulty identifying a clear direction. Participants reviewed the Ambassador Program Restructure 2026 document and examined the existing project outline to better understand expectations for project outcomes, milestones, and reporting. A key insight was the distinction between milestone reporting and output reporting, and the importance of having iterative progress assessments rather than waiting until the end of a project period to evaluate results

Several existing proposals from the Governance Working Group were reviewed, but there was a shared feeling that many of the ideas were not yet strong enough or sufficiently aligned with the program’s needs. This led to further ideation about alternative approaches listed in GovWG budget sheet https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1DeUB6ytoOeG6SOHwhJxe-vII_mXmkjdxG1iD-f5B2Sc/edit?gid=711478665#gid=711478665 and meetings notes here https://docs.google.com/document/d/10A9fiukKe_Da6LXD8qcnp5dLOjtbTpVIZ6ZehYoVsvA/edit?tab=t.q53ahkqkk74x

The discussion also explored alternative funding approaches, such as providing stipends to participants and allowing them to allocate recognition to contributors who supported their work—based on criteria like mentorship, marketing support, or succession capability. Participants noted that mentorship itself may not be a primary focus for the program going forward, and there was an ongoing challenge in defining the role the Ambassador Program wants to embody within the broader ecosystem.

Ultimately, the group recognised that selecting a project remains difficult because each has its own negative consequences and a lack of a clear outcome. We give ourselves more time to come up with ideas that would suit for more people or has clearer outcome. Some left with the feeling that we should just start a project to see how it goes.
