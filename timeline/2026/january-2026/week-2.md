#### Just Keep Going Inc
Kevin Frey gave an update on his organisation Just Keep Going Incorporated and its work using AI with athletes in Ghana. They recently signed a parnership agreement with Rejuve Biotech, and will be using their knowledge graph tool to build an athlete performance platform. Kevin noted that "it all started in these rooms" and the Ambassador program helped pave the way for his work.

#### Ecology/climate change and AI
New attendee Tina Shriver expressed interest in promoting ecological governance within AI systems. Brief discussion on issues around energy use, climate change, sustainability and CPU-intensive compute over GPU-intensive as a more energy-efficient approach; also using small-scale, open-source generative models on a CPU as opposed to larger-scale GPUs; and whether AI can be trained to give weight to ecological sustainability. Suggestion that the topic of sustainability and AI, and how we might experiment with ethical constraints on models, could be further discuused in the AI Ethics meeting next Monday.

#### Program restructure and budget issues:

There may also be targeted additional funding coming directly from the Foundation to support e.g. events etc. This could come with requirements, so that there isn't the complete freedom in how we use our budget that we have had until now
Suggestion of project-based funding  as opposed to workgroup-based to better align with this 

The need for strategic planning and collaboration, and importance of recognizing both successes and failures.
