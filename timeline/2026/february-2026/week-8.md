#### In this meeting we discussed:
- Meeting Coordination: lord Kizzy and Osmium welcomed the attendees, gave absent members a little more time to join in before laying down the discussion points on the topic Compute Is The New Power. Here are some of the discussion points
- Compute is the new bottle neck of intelligence: Lord Kizzy explained that at the moment in 2026, models are more easily accessible and open to everyone. With this in mind power isn’t necessarily equal to who has the best model but who controls compute because who controls it technically decides what to do with it. On a subtopic lord Kizzy explained how  ASI fixes this, he stated that  ASI reframes compute as a shared programmable decentralized intelligent substrate. Which means it isn’t hoarded by big tech companies and government bodies and people can earn from this which makes it market driven and also it allows for smaller teams to compete against large labs. 

- ASI compute models  and benefits: lord Kizzy explained that ASI models creates a decentralized and permissionless access to regional intelligence infrastructures an example NUNET which gives access to their GPU’s itself and everyone can earn form running GPU’s. The idea of allowing users access the GPU’s also speaks on the transparency side of decentralization.

- Decentralization as an architectural necessity: During the meeting lord Kizzy stated that single point control and centralized control equals to a kill switch which basically means that a single point of control translates to censorship and restrictions therefore, decentralized compute becomes a necessity. He stated that access to data and intelligence doesn’t need to be hoarded by big companies anymore it becomes something we all need to have access to so ASI comes into play by introducing a Fault- tolerant intelligence system, a cross regional redundancy whereby multiple regions can actually make use of it 

- Ownership and governance: It was evident during the meeting that each body considered to be in control of intelligence infrastructures had their cons and disadvantages for state being control and surveillance, big companies being monopolies and exploitation. Lord Kizzy suggested that ASI Compute Hybrid Answer is a programmable, governed compute with ownership being distributed across providers. The governance is going to be based on DAOs, agent-assisted oversight, and transparent rules, which is open and available to everyone.
